## Publishing checks
- [ ] Search intent matches the page type.
- [ ] Content is original and useful without the affiliate link.
- [ ] Product specs, prices, stock, and reviews are verified and dated where necessary.
- [ ] Affiliate relationships are disclosed near recommendations.
- [ ] Images are licensed/owned, compressed, responsive, and have meaningful alt text.
- [ ] Canonical, title, description, headings, and social preview are correct.
- [ ] Internal links work; no orphan page is created.
- [ ] Structured data matches visible page content exactly.
- [ ] Mobile layout and keyboard navigation are checked.
